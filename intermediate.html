How to Apply

1

Visit Official Portal

Open the official CHTE Balochistan Admission Portal.

2

Submit Online Application

Complete your online application carefully.

3

Select Your Programme

Choose Government Boys Degree College, Dera Allah Yar and your desired programme.

4

Merit List

Wait for the official merit list announcement.

5

Admission Confirmation

Documents

Required Documents

Please bring the following documents at the time of admission.

Matric Mark Sheet

Original and photocopy.

B-Form / CNIC

Original and photocopy.

Photographs

Recent passport-size photographs.

Character Certificate

From the last attended institution.

Important Instructions

  • Admissions are strictly on merit.
  • Applicants must first apply through the official CHTE Admission Portal.
  • Incomplete applications will not be considered.
  • Original documents must be produced during verification.
  • The college reserves the right to accept or reject any application according to Government rules.
